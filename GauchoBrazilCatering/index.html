About Gaucho Brazil Catering

We are dedicated to bringing the authentic taste of Brazilian barbecue to Parkland, FL. With our slow-roasted meats, rich flavors, and passionate service, we ensure your event—whether it's a small gathering or a large celebration—is unforgettable.

Menu Highlights

Brazilian BBQ

Picanha, Ribs, Sausage, Chicken wrapped in bacon, slow-cooked over a wood fire for that authentic churrasco flavor.

Meat & Shrimp Paella

A rich, saffron-infused rice dish loaded with succulent shrimp and savory meats, perfect for large parties.

Traditional Sides

Farofa, black beans (Feijoada), potato salad, and fresh vinaigrette to complement your meats perfectly.
